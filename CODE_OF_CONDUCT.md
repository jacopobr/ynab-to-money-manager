# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give and accept useful feedback without personal attacks.
- Protect other people's privacy, especially financial information.
- Focus discussions on what is best for the project and its users.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and other unprofessional conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through GitHub's repository owner contact or security reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
